COMPANY OVERVIEW

Company Story

Where Nature Meets Innovation
Every journey starts with a vision.

In 2023, Shree Salasar Balaji Minerals was founded with a single purpose—to redefine quality standards in India's mineral manufacturing industry.

Nestled in Makrana, Nagaur, a region celebrated worldwide for its rich marble reserves, we recognized an opportunity to transform premium natural minerals into products that drive industrial innovation.

What began as a passionate initiative has rapidly evolved into a trusted manufacturing brand, serving businesses across diverse industries with precision-engineered mineral solutions.

Every bag of mineral powder we produce carries our promise of purity, precision, and performance.

Today, we continue to build lasting partnerships by delivering products our customers can trust—every shipment, every order, every time.
