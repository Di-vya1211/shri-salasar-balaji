Frequently Asked Questions

Shree Salasar Balaji Minerals Group was established in 2018 and has grown into one of India's leading manufacturers and suppliers of industrial minerals and chemicals, backed by an experienced leadership team and a production capacity of roughly 200,000 tonnes annually.

Every batch is produced in our well-equipped manufacturing units under strict, hygienic operating conditions and rigorous quality-control processes. We follow set industrial standards at every stage, and use the latest production technology to maintain reliable purity and consistency across large volumes.

Packaging is handled through our dedicated packaging division, Salasar Addpack, which offers tailored packaging solutions to suit different product types, order volumes, and shipping requirements.

We supply both domestically and internationally. Beyond our India operations, Salasar International extends our reach to overseas markets including Vietnam, supporting clients who need reliable, large-scale mineral and chemical supply outside India.

We manufacture and supply a wide range of industrial minerals including Micronized Calcium Carbonate Powder, White Marble Powder, Natural Calcite Powder, Ground Calcium Carbonate Powder, Uncoated Calcite Powder, and Surface Treated Calcite Powder, catering to industries such as construction, plastics, paints, and more.
